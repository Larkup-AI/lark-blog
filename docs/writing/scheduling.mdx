---
title: Scheduled generation
---

<Info>Scheduling is on the roadmap (see `DECISIONS.md`). Basic schedules will be
in the open-source core; approval workflows and content calendars are Enterprise.</Info>

You'll define a cadence, a topic/prompt, a model and whether posts publish
automatically or land as drafts in a review queue. A `/api/cron/tick` endpoint
(protected by `CRON_SECRET`) is triggered by Vercel Cron or a Docker sidecar.
