---
title: Enterprise
---

Enterprise features live under `ee/` and unlock with a `LICENSE_KEY` (all
included on Cloud). Planned:

- **RBAC & custom roles** — fine-grained permissions per workspace.
- **SSO** — SAML and OIDC, domain verification, enforced SSO.
- **SCIM** — automated user provisioning.
- **Audit log** — who did what, with export.
- **Advanced scheduling** — multiple schedules, approval workflows, calendars.

The core is fully functional without a license key.
