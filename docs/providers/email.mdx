---
title: Email
---

Set `EMAIL_PROVIDER` to `resend`, `smtp`, or `none` (inferred if unset).

- **resend** — `RESEND_API_KEY`, `EMAIL_FROM`.
- **smtp** — `SMTP_HOST/PORT/USER/PASS/SECURE` (`pnpm add nodemailer`).
- **none** — email disabled. Invites become copyable links and password reset
  links are logged for an operator to relay. The app stays fully usable.
