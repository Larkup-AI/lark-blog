title: No-code embed
---

<Info>The embed script is on the roadmap. Target usage:</Info>

```html
<div id="lark-blog"></div>
<script src="https://cdn.lark.blog/embed.js" data-key="pk_..."></script>
```

Drop these two lines into Wix, Webflow, Framer, WordPress or Shopify and your
blog renders with no build step. For best SEO, use the Next.js integration or a
hosted blog on your own subdomain instead.
